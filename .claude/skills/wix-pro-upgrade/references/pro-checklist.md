# Professional-site checklist and scoring rubric

Score each area 1–5. **4** = professional, nothing embarrassing. **5** = best-in-class for the vertical. Anything ≤3 needs a specific finding with evidence.

## 1 · First impression (above the fold)
| Score | Looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | Template default text, no clear headline, stock hero unrelated to the business |
| 3 | Real headline but vague ("Welcome to our website"), CTA buried or absent |
| 5 | In three seconds a stranger knows what you do, for whom, and what to click next |
Checks: headline states the offer + audience; sub-headline states the benefit; one primary CTA; hero image is specific to this business; logo visible; nav ≤ 7 items.

## 2 · Message & copy
Checks: value proposition on the homepage; each page has one job; benefits before features; concrete numbers where true; consistent tone; no spelling errors; no lorem/placeholder; no walls of text (paragraphs ≤ 4 lines); CTAs use verbs ("Book a consultation", not "Submit"); contact and location where a visitor expects them.

## 3 · Visual identity
Checks: one logo, correct aspect, transparent background; ≤ 3 brand colours + neutrals used consistently; ≤ 2 type families, clear hierarchy (H1 > H2 > body); consistent spacing scale; aligned grids; images share a colour grade / style; buttons look identical everywhere; favicon set; mobile layout checked on ≤ 400 px width.

## 4 · Imagery
Checks: hero ≥ 1920 px wide, sharp; no visible watermarks or stock-photo tells (generic handshake, fake smiles); product/service photos on a consistent background; real team/premises photos where trust matters; every image has alt text; consistent aspect ratios per section; total page weight reasonable (no 8 MB PNGs).

## 5 · SEO & discoverability
Checks: unique `<title>` (≤ 60 chars) and meta description (≤ 155 chars) per page; one H1 per page; descriptive URLs; OG image + title for social sharing; sitemap submitted; robots not blocking; Google Business Profile linked (local business); structured data where Wix supports it (products, events, articles); no duplicate pages; 404 page branded.

## 6 · Structure & navigation
Checks: every page reachable in ≤ 2 clicks; nav labels are nouns visitors use, not internal jargon; footer has contact, legal, social, sitemap-lite; no dead links; consistent header/footer on all pages; breadcrumbs on deep sites; search if > 30 pages or a store.

## 7 · Business capability
Checks against the stated goal:
- Leads → form with ≤ 5 fields, confirmation message, notification set, spam protection.
- Sales → store configured (currency, tax, shipping, payments live), product pages complete (title, ≥ 3 photos, description, price, variants), cart + checkout tested.
- Bookings → services, durations, prices, staff, availability, reminders, payment on booking.
- Content → blog with ≥ 3 real posts, categories, author, share buttons.
- Members → login flow works, gated content actually gated.
Empty or half-configured apps score 1.

## 8 · Trust & credibility
Checks: physical address / registration where relevant; phone + email that work; real testimonials with names (owner-supplied only); client/partner logos (owner-supplied only); privacy policy + terms; SSL padlock; accurate business hours; social links that resolve; "About" page with real people.

## 9 · Accessibility
Checks: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 body text; alt text on informative images; heading order logical; visible focus states; form fields labelled; no text in images for critical info; captions/transcripts on video; language attribute set; RTL correct for Arabic.

## 10 · Performance & hygiene
Checks: no autoplay video with sound; ≤ 2 web fonts; images compressed; unused apps removed; no third-party widgets that slow load; analytics connected (Wix Analytics + Google); cookie banner if required; site name and description set in site properties; correct time zone and currency.
